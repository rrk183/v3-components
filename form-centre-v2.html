Getting started

What you need to start trading in the UAE.

To open a Corporate and Institutional Banking or Brokerage account with Emirates NBD Securities, please have the following documents ready.

  • Memorandum & Article of Association and all amendments to it, if any
  • Trade License
  • Commercial Registration
  • Registration with Chamber of Commerce
  • List of current Board Members
  • Authorized signatory letter issued by the company, to open and operate the account with ENBDS
  • List of Shareholders, providing the break-up of shareholders holding more than 5% shares
  • Passports or identity cards of authorized officials
  • All documents executed outside the UAE, notarized and attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of that country and the UAE embassy of that country, and also stamped by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the UAE
