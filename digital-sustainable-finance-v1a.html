How we work

From framework to execution.

Four ways our team helps clients raise sustainable capital and move their sustainability agenda forward.

01 · Structuring

Green, Social and Sustainability-linked instruments.

Structuring of green, social and sustainability-linked loans, bonds and Sukuk, including the creation of frameworks and advice on use of proceeds in line with best practice.

02 · Targets

KPIs and sustainability performance targets.

Designing KPIs and SPTs for sustainability-linked loans, bonds and Sukuk, helping clients take positive steps towards greater sustainability.

03 · Transition

A sustainable, low-carbon future.

Enabling clients to transition to a sustainable and low-carbon future, with financing structured around the milestones of that journey.

04 · Advisory

Strategic change on the sustainability journey.

Advising clients on positive strategic change management on their sustainability journey, from first commitments to published outcomes.

Digital finance

Technology-enabled innovation, across the financing lifecycle.

Digital & Sustainable Finance provides integrated digital and sustainable finance solutions, combining technology-enabled innovation with ESG expertise to support clients’ strategic, financing and transition objectives. The team works with every Emirates NBD Capital product line, so a digital or sustainable structure can be brought into a bond, a Sukuk, a syndicated facility or an equity transaction.
