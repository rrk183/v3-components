  • Fast, secure payment acceptance across MENAT

  • Award-winning gateway (Best Payment Gateway, MEA)

Emirates NBD × Partior

Emirates NBD is redefining cross-border payments with Partior, a multi-currency, blockchain-based clearing and settlement network. Emirates NBD has enabled the region’s first real-time blockchain-based cross-border USD payments on the Partior network, and is advancing towards connecting with other banks across further currencies, strengthening its payments offering through faster settlement and shorter turnaround times for clients.

  • First bank in the region to enable real-time, blockchain-based cross-border payments
  • Concurrent beneficiary pre-validation and compliance screening ensures payment certainty
  • Faster, more seamless payment flows for clients
“Our partnership with Partior is a critical element of our broader strategy to embrace new technologies.” Ahmed Al Qassim, Group Head of Wholesale Banking

Emirates NBD × iPiD

Emirates NBD partnered with iPiD, a leading payee-validation provider, to verify beneficiary account details in real time before a cross-border payment is sent to supported corridors, strengthening our cross-border payment infrastructure.

  • Validation of Payee for major corridors
  • Reduces fraud, delays and returned payments
  • Greater confidence and new FX opportunities

Emirates NBD Pay × Kinexys (by J.P. Morgan)

Emirates NBD joined Kinexys Liink, J.P. Morgan’s bank-led peer-to-peer network, integrating the Confirm application to validate UAE account information globally before payments are initiated.

  • Validates account ownership pre-payment
  • Reduces misdirected payments and returns
  • Positions Emirates NBD as a regional leader in payment security
