Project Finance & Contracting

Our Project Finance team delivers tailored long-term financing solutions, including ECA-backed lending, for complex infrastructure, strategic projects and public-private partnerships (PPPs) to achieve sustainable outcomes. Building on our strong presence in the UAE, KSA, Egypt, Turkey, India, Singapore and London, we partner with key stakeholders including sponsors, developers, national champions and multinational companies, supporting their ambitions from key anchor markets into global opportunities.

Complementing this infrastructure development, the Contracting team works hand in hand with local and multinational specialist EPC contractors and supply-chain participants, delivering customised financing solutions that draw on deep sector knowledge and rigorous structuring to support the successful execution and delivery of major projects.

Our strengths

Sector breadthActive across a wide array of infrastructure projects: airports, ports, railways, metro systems, roads and tunnels, oil and gas both upstream and downstream, power generation, desalination, water treatment, renewable energy, and residential and commercial complexes, in addition to sustainable finance (ESG) funding.
RelationshipsOur established relationships with governments, sponsors, developers, local and international specialist EPC contractors, financial institutions and key industry stakeholders enable early engagement, efficient structuring and effective syndication.
Scale & structuring expertiseOur underwriting capacity and structuring capabilities, together with value-enhancing roles such as Account Bank, Facility Agent and ESG Green Coordinator, our distribution network and execution experience, position us to support complex, large-scale projects through their development, construction and operational phases, covering multi-billion-dollar transactions.

Our advantage

With a leading regional franchise and established footprints in the UAE, KSA, Egypt, Turkey, India, Singapore and London, landmark transaction experience and an extensive network of relationships, we provide clients with dependable execution, coordinated financing solutions and access to a broad pool of capital, serving as a platform for international growth.
