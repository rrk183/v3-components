Sustainable Finance

Financing the green-economy transition across MENAT.

We partner with corporates, financial institutions and public entities to accelerate decarbonisation, diversify portfolios and drive social impact, through green, sustainability-linked and transition-finance solutions.

Solutions

What we offer

Four ways to fund and manage your transition, each built to recognised market principles.

Use-of-Proceeds

Green, social and sustainability-labelled loans and bonds, with proceeds ring-fenced for eligible projects.

Sustainability-Linked

General-purpose financing where pricing is linked to your ESG KPIs and Sustainability Performance Targets.

Transition Finance

Solutions for hard-to-abate sectors with a credible, science-based decarbonisation pathway.

Sustainable Treasury & Liquidity

Green deposits, ESG investment notes and hedging wrappers.

Success story

Our debut USD 750mn Green Bond.

In 2023, Emirates NBD priced this USD 750 million Green Bond, the largest from a MENAT-region bank at the time of issuance. Issued under the bank’s Sustainable Finance Framework, it funds eligible projects across renewable energy, green buildings, clean transport and water management.

Making change happen

A KPI & target framework that drives transformation.

We help you set material KPIs that are measurable, time-bound and externally verifiable.

Intensity or absolute targets

e.g. tCO₂ per unit of output, or % renewable energy by 2030.

Margin step-ups and step-downs

Triggered by target achievement, defined in the documentation.

Annual independent verification

Progress is published in full each year.
