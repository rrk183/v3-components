Trade Finance

Letters of Credit

A bank-backed promise that your supplier gets paid only after they ship the goods and present the agreed documents.

  • Import LC and Export LC, issued or confirmed
  • Payment released only against compliant shipping documents
  • Confirmation adds a second bank's payment assurance
  • Can finance the payment term to ease cash flow

For importers and exporters who need payment certainty across borders.

Talk to our trade team
Trade Finance

Bank Guarantees

A bank undertaking to pay your counterparty if your business fails to perform or pay under a contract.

  • Financial guarantees securing a payment obligation
  • Performance and advance payment guarantees
  • Standby letters of credit as a payment backstop
  • Issued outward, and received inward

For businesses bidding or contracting where trust is not yet established.

Talk to our trade team
Trade Finance

Import & Export Collections

Banks exchange the shipping documents for payment, so the buyer takes title to the goods only when the terms are met.

  • Exporter's bank collects through the importer's bank
  • Documents released against payment or acceptance
  • Available for both import and export flows
  • Suits established, trusted trading relationships

For partners who trust each other but want bank-controlled document release.

Talk to our trade team
Trade Finance

Shipping Guarantees

Lets you collect imported goods from the carrier before the original shipping documents arrive, avoiding delays at port.

  • Bank guarantees the carrier so goods release early
  • Issued together with a delivery order
  • Avoids storage and demurrage costs at port
  • Commonly used under an import LC or collection

For importers whose goods arrive before the bill of lading.

Talk to our trade team
Trade Finance

Trade Loans

Short-term financing that bridges the gap between paying for your goods and getting paid by your buyer.

  • Import loans via Loan Against Trust Receipt
  • Post-shipment finance against goods already shipped
  • Frees up working capital and improves cash flow
  • Cross-border and domestic trade, multiple currencies

For importers and exporters managing working-capital gaps.

Talk to our trade team
