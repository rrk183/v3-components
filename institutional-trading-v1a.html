How can we help

How we approach every order.

How we execute matters as much as where. Five principles shape every mandate, from the connectivity behind your flow to the desk behind your instructions.

1 / 5

Order routing and control

Reach only matters if orders behave well once they arrive. We keep latency low, route flow to the right book and leave the choice of path in your hands.

  • The path to each venue stays your decision, not ours
  • Flow is directed without unnecessary delay or detours
  • One connection serves your full range of order types and strategies

Automation and transparency

By linking your OMS to our execution platform over FIX, we automate the workflow end to end and keep reporting aligned with your internal systems.

  • Automated processing from pre-trade through to post-trade
  • Customisable fields and reporting that map to your systems
  • Faster flows for programme trades and portfolio rebalancing
  • Built for systematic strategies and fast-moving markets

Disciplined risk control

Confidentiality is treated as part of best execution, not an afterthought. Robust controls and operational rigour protect your intentions and your fills at every stage.

  • Execution structured to limit signalling and market impact
  • Rigorous controls that uphold confidentiality and compliance
  • Systems engineered to stay available and perform when markets move fast

High-touch desk service

Assigned relationship managers handle onboarding and commercial discussions, while the trading desk stays available for execution instructions and strategy.

  • One point of contact who knows your mandate and your flow
  • Ongoing dialogue on approach, conditions and liquidity
  • Coordinated support with Emirates NBD Group teams where relevant

Block and complex execution

For large, illiquid or advanced orders, we design execution case by case, balancing size, urgency and price objectives against the liquidity available on the day.

  • Negotiated blocks and carefully staged orders
  • Crossing opportunities between natural buyers and sellers where permissible
  • Intelligent slicing, timing and venue selection in line with your preferences
  • Support for event-driven flows, index changes and rebalances
