Card surfaces

One look class, one paint class, one finish — every card in the system. The finish modifiers carry no colour at all: surfaces own the fill and the ink, is-* owns the border and the shadow. An unpainted card-panel is a light island — put it on navy and its content keeps light-mode ink automatically.

Finish × paint are independent — the same three finishes (raised · tile · flat) over three surfaces
