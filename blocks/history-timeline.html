Our history

Four decades shaping
banking in the region.

From a single Dubai bank to a global group serving over fourteen million customers worldwide.

1997

Emirates Bank International founded

Emirates Bank International was established in Dubai, laying the foundation for what would become one of the region's most trusted banking institutions.

2007

Historic merger creates Emirates NBD

Emirates Bank International and National Bank of Dubai merged to form Emirates NBD, creating the UAE's largest bank by assets and a regional powerhouse.

2013

Launch of businessONLINE

Emirates NBD launched businessONLINE, its flagship corporate digital banking platform, enabling businesses to manage payments and liquidity across 180+ countries.

2019

Acquisition of DenizBank

Emirates NBD completed the landmark acquisition of DenizBank in Turkey, significantly expanding its global presence and customer base to over 14 million.

2022

Digital banking leadership

Emirates NBD was recognised as the Most Innovative Bank in the Middle East, with over 90% of transactions conducted digitally across its platforms.

2024

AI-powered banking platform

Launched next-generation AI-driven financial tools including ENBD X and businessONLINE X, delivering personalised banking experiences for millions of customers.

2025

Simpler. Better. Smarter.

Emirates NBD continues to lead digital transformation with its ecosystem of tools — businessONLINE, smartTRADE, Emirates NBD Pay and smartSCF — serving corporate clients across 13 countries.
