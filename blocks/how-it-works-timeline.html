How it works

From sign up to reconciled reporting, in five steps.

One agreement, one channel, then day-to-day messaging that stays standardised.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Agree a product service agreement with Emirates NBD through your Relationship Manager.

  2. 2

    Connect via SCORE

    Join the SWIFT closed user group that links corporates to banks.

  3. 3

    Send instructions

    Push single MT messages over SWIFT FIN, or files and batches over SWIFT FileACT.

  4. 4

    Receive and reconcile

    Pull statements and reporting from multiple banks through the one channel.

  5. 5

    Track status

    Get network ACK or NACK on FIN messages, and response messages such as pain.002 on FileACT.
