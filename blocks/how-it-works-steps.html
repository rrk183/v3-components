How it works

From first step to fully set up, in four moves.

A guided journey, not a single transaction. Here is how it comes together and how you stay in control once it is live.

  1. 01

    Know your eligibility

    We assess your portfolio and history against clear criteria, then assign an approved limit based on the value and classification of your holdings and your overall profile.

    • Portfolio and history reviewed against defined criteria
    • Limit set by the value and quality of your collateral
    • A suitability assessment that confirms it is right for you
  2. 02

    Set up your account

    Once approved, a dedicated account is created and linked to your existing profile, with your eligible assets and balances recognised automatically.

    • A dedicated account, linked to your existing profile
    • Eligible assets and cash recognised automatically
    • No separate paperwork to arrange
  3. 03

    Put it to work

    Use your new capacity across the approved universe. Everything updates automatically as prices and values change, so your position is always current.

    • Act across the full approved list
    • Values recalculated in real time
    • Build or diversify without unwinding what you hold
  4. 04

    Stay in control

    Real-time tools show your utilisation, coverage and headroom, so there are no surprises. Clear triggers and transparent communication keep you ahead of every move.

    • Live view of utilisation, coverage and headroom
    • Defined triggers and transparent communication
    • Clear actions whenever conditions change
