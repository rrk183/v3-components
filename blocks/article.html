OPEC+ sticks with incremental increases in output

EB
Edward Bell Acting Group Head of Research and Chief Economist
20 November 2025 2 min read

A steady hand on output buys OPEC+ time, but the outlook for oil market balances still points to a substantial inventory build.

Targets held steady into Q1 2026

For the first time this year OPEC+ set out production targets for longer than a month ahead, agreeing to increase production by 137k b/d for December and then hold targets at those levels until the end of Q1 2026.
