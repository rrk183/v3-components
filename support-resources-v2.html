Fraud awareness

Spot it. Report it. Stop it.

Fraud tactics grow more convincing every day, and businesses are a prime target. Recognising the warning signs early is what stops an attack before it lands.

Visit the Fraud Awareness Hub →

Suspect or experiencing a fraud incident? Call Emirates NBD immediately on 600 54 0000, keep all related evidence, and notify your IT team and the relevant authorities. Emirates NBD will never ask you for passwords, passcodes, or SmartPass approvals.
