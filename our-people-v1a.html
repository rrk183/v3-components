Group Head of Equity Capital Markets & Advisory

Prasad Chari

Years of experience: 25+ years

Prasad has been instrumental in the development of the UAE capital markets and currently heads the Equity Capital Markets and Advisory team at Emirates NBD Capital.

Before joining Emirates NBD in 2011, Prasad was with Macquarie Capital for over 4 years advising on several infrastructure and project finance deals, including the first UAE PPPs with ZonesCorp.

Prior to that, Prasad worked with KPMG for over 9 years in the Corporate Finance and Assurance teams in the UAE and UK primarily covering fund raising, corporate finance advisory and M&A.

In addition to spearheading some of the most high profile transactions in the region, Prasad leads origination efforts across the GCC, US, UK, and India and has strong relationships with investors across the MENATSA region.

Key responsibilities: Deal origination, investor coverage and project leadership.

Notable transactions: IPOs / FMO: USD 2.0bn Talabat IPO, USD 1.0bn Salik IPO, USD 858m du selldown by Mubadala, USD 6.1bn DEWA. M&A:  USD 400m Sale of National Taxi to Dubai Taxi Company, USD 235m Capital Raise for Emirates Rawabi, Tender Offers: USD 2.1bn Voluntary Tender Offer by L’Imad on AD Ports, USD 485m Voluntary Tender Offer by ADQ on Aramex.

Education and qualifications: Prasad holds an MBA from Nottingham University Business School, UK and has an FCCA from UK.
