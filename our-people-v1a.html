Group Head, ECM & Advisory

Prasad Chari

With over two decades of experience at blue-chip multinationals across sectors and geographies, Prasad has been instrumental in the development of the UAE capital markets and heads the Equity Capital Markets platform for Emirates NBD. Before Emirates NBD he spent over four years at Macquarie Capital advising on infrastructure and project finance, including the first UAE PPPs, and nine years at KPMG in Corporate Finance and Assurance across the UAE and UK.

Recent transactions: the IPOs of talabat, LuLu, DEWA, EMPOWER, SALIK, Taaleem, Network International, Emaar Development and Emaar Malls, and the USD 1.2bn private placement in GEMS Education.

Head of Debt Capital Markets

Ritesh Agarwal

Ritesh joined the DCM team at Emirates NBD Capital in 2014. He leads the origination, structuring and execution of Debt Capital Markets solutions, both conventional and Islamic, for sovereigns, supranationals, financial institutions and corporates.

He has helped clients across the GCC, Asia and Africa raise over USD 50bn across senior secured, unsecured and hybrid instruments. He previously worked in the Capital Markets team at Bank of America Merrill Lynch in Mumbai.

Head, Global Capital Markets Syndicate

Rahul Sethi, CFA

Rahul co-heads the debt syndicate desk at Emirates NBD Capital, which he joined in 2015 from RBS Singapore. He previously worked with the DCM Syndicate and Treasury team at Barclays Singapore, covering deal origination and syndication.

Rahul is a CFA charterholder and a Chartered Accountant from India.

Senior Director, Equity Capital Markets & Advisory

Lorenzo Vertechi

Lorenzo joined Emirates NBD Capital in 2022 with markets experience across sectors and products, including IPOs, capital raisings, accelerated transactions and equity-linked instruments. He was previously at Intesa Sanpaolo in London, leading ECM origination on high-profile 2021 transactions including the Allfunds IPO and the Vonovia rights issue.

Recent transactions: talabat, LuLu, Spinneys, Parkin, Investcorp Capital, Empower and Salik. He holds an MSc in Finance from Bocconi University, Milan.

Senior Director, Debt Capital Markets

Shayaan Khan

Shayaan has over 12 years of experience in Investment Banking and Global Markets across MENAT and Asia, focused on the origination, advisory and structuring of DCM instruments in conventional, Sukuk and ESG formats, having raised over USD 40bn for global clients.

He previously led the global structured loans syndicate desk at Emirates NBD and began his career at Bank of America Merrill Lynch in Hong Kong. He holds a BSc in Finance from the Kelley School of Business.
