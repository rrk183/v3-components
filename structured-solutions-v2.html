01 · Risk & Hedging Solutions

Navigate volatility with smarter hedging.

Interest-rate, currency and commodity swings can erode margins and disrupt planning. We structure hedges around your exposures, so you can lock in predictable costs, protect earnings and act on your market view with confidence.

Key products

Cross-Currency SwapsInterest-Rate SwapsCommodity HedgesZero-Cost Collars
Cross-currency swap

Interest-rate alignment

Challenge
A large regional conglomerate with multi-currency debt faced mismatched interest-rate exposures between USD and AED, creating earnings volatility as global rates rose.
Our solution
We structured a five-year USD 1bn to AED cross-currency swap to convert the client's USD floating-rate liabilities into AED-denominated cash flows. The swap aligned both currencies' interest benchmarks and synchronised payment cycles.
Impact
The client achieved full rate and currency alignment, reduced P&L volatility, and gained predictable funding costs for long-term capital projects, strengthening their credit profile and building investor confidence.
Cross-currency swap

Removing FX risk from foreign-currency debt

Challenge
A corporate borrower's USD loan exposed repayments to currency movements, adding uncertainty to its debt-service costs.
Our solution
We structured an AED 2bn USD/AED cross-currency swap, converting the exposure into a local-currency position with flexible settlement timing and call options for early unwinding.
Impact
The client removed currency risk from its debt portfolio and now manages liquidity entirely in dirhams, sharpening financial planning and return calculations on new investments.
Commodity hedge

Oil hedge for an aviation client

Challenge
A leading Middle-Eastern airline faced jet-fuel price volatility, threatening operational budgets and long-term route planning as energy markets fluctuated sharply.
Our solution
We structured a multi-year jet-fuel hedging programme using swaps and zero-cost option collars, tailored to the airline's fuel consumption profile and accounting needs. Our commodities desk actively monitored market spreads and adjusted strike levels to maintain budget discipline while taking advantage of favourable price movements.
Impact
Our hedging programme stabilised fuel costs over multiple years, strengthened earnings predictability, and protected competitive ticket pricing. The airline preserved profit margins through double-digit oil volatility.
02 · Financing & Liquidity Solutions

Free up liquidity with asset-backed, structured financing.

In fast-moving markets, businesses need flexible funding avenues that preserve working capital, support growth, and reduce reliance on traditional lending. We shape structured financing around your business and assets, enabling efficient capital access while maintaining operational momentum.

Key products

Structured LoansRepoTotal Return SwapsGold-Linked FinancingExchange-for-Swaps (EFS)
Exchange-for-Swaps

EFS financing for an agricultural client

Challenge
A regional agri-commodities firm actively hedged price risk through exchange-traded futures, but escalating margin requirements tied up substantial working capital, restricting the company's ability to expand procurement and production.
Our solution
We structured an Exchange-for-Swaps (EFS) financing arrangement, enabling the client to transfer exchange futures positions to the bank in return for equivalent OTC swaps, without the need for daily margin posting.
Impact
The structure released significant liquidity back into core operations, improved balance-sheet efficiency, and maintained uninterrupted hedge protection.
Gold-linked financing

Liquidity against physical bullion

Challenge
A commodity trader required short-term liquidity whilst holding substantial physical gold inventory, but preferred to retain price-exposure rather than liquidate.
Our solution
We structured a gold-backed financing facility secured against physical bullion, with repayment indexed to the underlying market value.
Impact
Unlocked USD 50mn in flexible funding, maintained full upside exposure, and optimised financing costs relative to unsecured borrowing, supporting the client's regional expansion.
Repo

Structured repo financing

Challenge
A corporate treasury team sought short-term liquidity to fund strategic initiatives without selling their fixed-income holdings.
Our solution
We delivered a repo facility supported by high-quality bonds, offering efficient liquidity with flexible terms and margining.
Impact
Improved liquidity runway, enhanced treasury risk management, and preserved investment performance.
