Shariah-compliant Financing

Financing structured to fit how you grow.

From asset leasing to profit-sharing partnerships, scroll through the structure that fits your business, backed by strong governance and clear performance monitoring.

1 / 7

Ijarah

Ijarah provides medium- and long-term financing for capital assets by allowing you to lease equipment, machinery, or vehicles without large upfront payments. We purchase the asset you select, lease it to you for an agreed term, and transfer ownership once payments are complete.

  • Structured asset leasing, where Emirates NBD purchases the asset on your behalf and leases it to you over an agreed period, with ownership transferring to you once all payments are completed.
  • Suitable for durable assets that remain intact through use, such as equipment, vehicles, and other non-consumable capital goods.

Forward Ijarah

Forward Ijarah enables you to finance assets under construction through a Shariah-compliant structure that links a future sale with a forward lease. We fund the developer during production, and once the asset is completed it is leased back to you under an agreed payment plan, providing certainty throughout the process.

  • Medium- to long-term financing structures primarily for construction of infrastructure projects including homes, offices, and plants.
  • The completed asset is leased to you for a specific period; upon lease completion, asset ownership is transferred to you.
  • Lease amounts must be known on the contract date.
  • Provides flexibility, as payments can be made in installments linked to project completion.
  • Emirates NBD undertakes to pay the contracted developer or builder in full, or in specific stages.

Murabaha Finance

Murabaha is a sale-based financing structure in which Emirates NBD purchases a specified asset at your request, assuming ownership risk and then sells it to you at a disclosed cost-plus-profit on a deferred payment basis, providing transparent, Shariah-compliant financing without conventional lending.

  • Suitable for businesses of all sizes, from SMEs to large corporates.
  • An effective way to purchase a wide range of eligible assets, including raw materials, semi-finished and finished goods, or store and spare requirements.

Musharaka

Musharaka is a partnership structure where both Emirates NBD and the client contribute capital, share profits per agreed ratios, and absorb losses in line with the original investment contribution. Depending on the financing required, Emirates NBD would become a partner in your business or a defined segment, underpinned by strong governance and clear performance-monitoring.

  • Once all partners contribute capital to the joint pool, the Musharaka's assets become jointly owned.
  • Each partner may participate in managing the business.
  • Losses are shared by all partners in proportion to their invested capital.

Tawarruq

Meet your liquidity needs, even without business assets, through Shariah-compliant monetisation. Tawarruq offers a flexible way to access working capital and longer-term financing. Emirates NBD would purchase commodities from a supplier and sell them on to you, so that you can generate the liquidity you need by selling those commodities on to another party.

  • Meets all types of Shariah-compliant financing needs.
  • Flexible terms and repayment schedules.

Wakala

Wakala is an agency arrangement where Emirates NBD appoints the client to execute agreed Shariah-compliant investments to generate expected returns. Profits are shared according to a pre-agreed ratio, with the wakil managing the investments on the bank's behalf and retaining any excess return as an added incentive.

  • Profit is allocated based on the agreed ratio.
  • Expected returns are outlined by the bank.
  • The client executes the investment to realise those returns.

Mudaraba Finance

Mudaraba Finance is a partnering arrangement where Emirates NBD, as Rab Al Mal, supplies capital to a business or Mudarib, to invest in a defined commercial venture. Profits are shared under an agreed ratio, with losses absorbed by the bank unless the client is negligent, engages in misconduct, or commits fraud.

  • Used for term finance and working capital needs.
  • Provides flexibility in terms of utilization of funds and terms.
  • Pre-agreed profit ratio.

Good to know

Islamic deposits, answered.

An Islamic Fixed Deposit is a Shariah-compliant way to place your funds for a set period. Your money is invested in approved assets and you receive profit based on the performance of those investments, following structures such as Mudaraba or Wakala. No interest is paid at any point, and all activity is overseen by the bank’s Internal Shariah Supervision Committee.

Emirates NBD Islamic offers two main types of deposits. Mudaraba deposits work on a profit-sharing basis where the bank manages your funds in Shariah-compliant investments. Wakala deposits work through an agency arrangement where the bank invests your funds on your behalf with an expected profit rate. Both options follow Shariah guidelines and differ mainly in how profit is calculated.

You can open an Islamic Fixed Deposit once you have an active account with Emirates NBD Islamic. The process can be completed through a branch or using the bank’s digital channels. You choose the amount, the currency and the term, then review and sign the Shariah-compliant contract. After the bank confirms your request, the funds are placed for the agreed period.

The minimum amount depends on the type of deposit you choose. Mudaraba deposits usually start from AED 10,000 or the equivalent in USD. Wakala deposits are designed for larger placements and start from AED 4mn or USD 1mn. These thresholds may vary and should be confirmed with the bank at the time of application.

Yes, Islamic Fixed Deposits are generally available in AED and USD. Availability may vary depending on the specific deposit structure you choose. If you require another currency, you can check with the bank to confirm whether it is currently offered under a Shariah-compliant structure.
