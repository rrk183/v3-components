What is Margin Trading

Borrow against your holdings, instead of selling them.

Margin Trading lets you use eligible securities and cash in your portfolio as collateral for an investment credit line. That credit line multiplies your buying power, funds new or larger positions and frees up liquidity, all while you stay invested in the holdings you already own.

Key features

Built for serious, experienced investors.

A facility designed around the way you already invest, with competitive pricing, a fully digital application and quick access to funds once you are approved.

Greater buying power

Take larger or additional positions than your own funds would allow, with a credit line that scales to the value of your eligible collateral.

Fully digital application

Apply online and move from eligibility check to an active margin line without the paperwork of a traditional process.

Competitive interest rates

Transparent, competitively priced financing so more of your potential return stays with you rather than the cost of borrowing.

Broad approved universe

Trade against an approved basket of shares listed across ADX, DFM and Tadawul, spanning the region's most active names.

No share pledge required

Your holdings act as collateral within your account, with no separate share pledge to set up and no extra paperwork to manage.

Cash-out flexibility

Draw cash of up to 50% of your approved limit, subject to eligibility criteria, to fund needs beyond the market.

Dedicated desk support

Work with a Securities team that knows your portfolio and helps you manage utilisation, coverage and margin calls.

How it works

From eligibility to active buying power, in five steps.

Margin Trading is a guided journey, not a single transaction. Here is how a facility comes together at Emirates NBD and how you stay in control once it is live.

  1. 01

    Know your eligibility

    We assess your portfolio and trading history against clear eligibility criteria, then assign an approved facility limit based on the value and classification of your securities and your overall risk profile.

    • Portfolio and trading history reviewed against defined criteria
    • Facility limit set by the value and quality of your collateral
    • A suitability assessment that confirms margin is right for you
  2. 02

    Set up your margin line

    Once approved, a dedicated margin account is created and linked to your Emirates NBD Securities trading account, with your eligible shares and cash balances acting as collateral.

    • A dedicated margin account, linked to your trading account
    • Eligible shares and cash recognised as collateral
    • No separate share pledge to arrange
  3. 03

    Put your buying power to work

    Use your margin line to purchase additional shares from the approved universe across ADX, DFM and Tadawul. Your purchasing power updates automatically as market prices and collateral values change.

    • Buy additional shares from the approved marginable list
    • Purchasing power recalculated in real time
    • Build or diversify positions without selling existing holdings
  4. 04

    Stay in control as markets move

    Real-time tools show your utilisation, coverage ratio and proximity to margin-call levels, so there are no surprises. If movements reduce your coverage below set thresholds, a margin call asks you to reduce exposure or add funds or securities.

    • Live view of utilisation, coverage and headroom
    • Defined triggers and transparent communication
    • Clear actions to restore coverage when markets move against you
  5. 05

    Manage or close your facility

    As you sell securities or inject cash, your outstanding margin balance reduces. The facility can be reduced, renewed or closed in line with your instructions and the contractual terms.

    • Outstanding balance falls as you sell or add cash
    • Reduce, renew or close the facility on your terms
    • Periodic reviews to keep the facility aligned with your strategy
