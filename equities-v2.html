Emirates NBD Securities, the leading brokerage and financial services company in the UAE, announced the launch of its trading platform on TADAWUL, the Saudi Stock Exchange. The addition of TADAWUL trading benefits eligible investors, who currently hold a regular trading account with Emirates NBD Securities, to trade on the Saudi exchange without having to request new settlement bank accounts.

The threat of US tariffs disrupting the outlook for the global economy, geopolitical volatility in the Middle East and uncertainty around interest rate policies from the Fed amid other dynamics have all formed a noisy backdrop for the performance of GCC assets.
