Category 01

Getting Started

This section guides you through establishing your corporate banking relationship with Emirates NBD. It outlines the onboarding steps, required documentation and the support available to ensure an efficient start whether you operate in the UAE, GCC or internationally.

Opening a corporate account with Emirates NBD is a structured, globally aligned process. You begin by selecting your current location and the jurisdiction where you require an account. The onboarding team coordinates the documentation set including incorporation papers, board resolutions and authorised signatory information. Multinational entities benefit from our presence across the GCC, Turkiye, Asia and Europe, enabling account opening even when your headquarters are outside the UAE. Corporate banking representatives remain available throughout the process, including a 24/7 service line.

Corporate trading accounts are offered through Emirates NBD Securities. You submit core corporate documents including constitutional documents, a valid trade licence, commercial registration, a board resolution and authorised signatory details. Once submitted, Emirates NBD Securities activates the account and provides access to UAE and regional markets. Digital onboarding tools support fast, compliant documentation handling for corporate and institutional investors.

Category 02

Digital Solutions

This section introduces our digital corporate banking platforms that help you enhance transparency, efficiency and control across global treasury, trade and payment operations.

businessONLINE is our global digital corporate banking ecosystem. It provides unified access to your accounts with optional external account reporting, enabling real-time oversight across regions. The platform supports cross-border payments, payroll, vendor flows, trade services and FX activity through one interface. You can monitor liquidity, configure dashboards and issue secure remote instructions. These capabilities strengthen visibility and operational governance for corporate treasurers and finance leads.

smartTRADE is our digital trade finance portal designed for global trade flows. You can initiate Letters of Credit, guarantees, and import and export documents online, supported by real-time status updates and digital document uploads. The platform reduces manual processing, improves cycle times, and enhances control for corporates managing international trade activity across the GCC, MENA, Europe and Asia.

smartSCF is our supply chain finance platform designed to optimise working capital for buyers and suppliers across global value chains. You can digitise payables, extend terms with comfort, and provide suppliers with early access to approved invoices at competitive rates without collateral. The platform offers real-time dashboards, automated invoice processing and optional dynamic discounting. It supports liquidity management for corporates operating regional and international supply networks.

Emirates NBD Pay is our payment acceptance platform for in-store and online channels. It supports card payments in more than 143 currencies and enables chip and PIN, contactless, QR and digital wallet transactions. For online businesses, Emirates NBD Pay offers secure e-commerce gateways with tokenisation, fraud controls and configurable checkout flows. You can issue payment links and track transactions through a unified dashboard. These capabilities support merchants serving global customer bases.

Category 04

Fraud & Awareness

Explore key fraud risks affecting corporates in the UAE and GCC, including phishing, invoice fraud, BEC, cyberattacks and payment redirection. Learn how to safeguard your treasury operations and protect digital banking activity with clear controls and governance steps.

Phishing occurs when attackers impersonate a trusted party to obtain your credentials, payment details or confidential information. Warning signs include urgent requests for login details, unusual sender addresses, and suspicious links or attachments. Always validate unexpected instructions through an independent channel before acting.

Invoice fraud uses fake, altered or duplicated invoices, often mimicking a genuine vendor, to trick you into paying a criminal. Reduce exposure by matching each invoice to a purchase order, scrutinising any change to a supplier's payment details, and applying maker-checker controls for beneficiary updates. A brief verification step protects high-value transactions.

BEC involves fraudulent or compromised email accounts used to request urgent payments or sensitive data from your team. Strengthen protection by confirming unusual or high-value instructions through a phone call, enabling dual approval for payments, and using strong authentication with regular access reviews. If an email feels out of pattern, pause and verify.

Insider threats arise when employees or contractors misuse access to manipulate data, divert funds or leak information. Mitigation includes restricting permissions to essential roles, using dual control for financial approvals, reviewing access rights regularly, and maintaining clear reporting channels for concerns. Controls and transparency reduce internal risk.

Ransomware, malware, credential theft and unauthorised access increasingly target corporate digital banking environments. Strengthen your defences by keeping systems and patches current, training employees to identify suspicious activity, enforcing strong authentication, and monitoring digital banking activity daily. Preparedness limits financial and operational impact.

When using businessONLINE, smartTRADE, smartSCF or Emirates NBD Pay, access platforms only through official URLs, review payment details before approval, apply role-based access, disable inactive users, and enable two-factor authentication. Avoid approving transactions over public Wi-Fi. Strong internal governance is your most effective safeguard.

Redirection fraud targets an existing supplier relationship: criminals quietly switch a trusted beneficiary's bank details to an account they control. Protect your payment flows by maintaining whitelisted beneficiaries, limiting who can amend bank details, and callback-verifying every change through a pre-agreed contact. Treat last-minute or urgent amendment requests as high-risk and verify before releasing funds.

Act immediately. Contact Emirates NBD Corporate Fraud Support on 600 54 0000. Pause approvals, block suspicious access, and preserve evidence such as emails, logs, invoices and screenshots. Alert your internal IT or security teams and notify regulators or authorities where required. Rapid escalation improves the likelihood of recovery.
