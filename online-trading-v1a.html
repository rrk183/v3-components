Overview

A platform built around how you trade.

Manage your investments from the web platform or the Securities mobile app and stay close to every opportunity, wherever you are. A clean, customisable interface brings markets, orders and analytics into a single view, so you can read the picture quickly and act with confidence.

What we offer

Everything you need to trade, in one place.

Seven capabilities that bring market data, execution and portfolio insight together, built around the way active investors work.

Real-time analytics & charting

Live prices, technical indicators and flexible timeframes that turn raw data into clearer decisions.

Fast order execution

Submit and track market, limit and other supported order types, then follow each one through to settlement.

Unified portfolio dashboard

Track holdings, cash positions and performance in a single dashboard you can read at a glance.

Synchronised watchlists

Build multiple watchlists that stay in step across the web platform and the Securities mobile app.

UAE & GCC market access

Reach DFM, ADX, Tadawul and other key GCC markets through a single, secure login.

Configurable layouts

Arrange screens and widgets to match your trading style for fast, comfortable day-to-day use.

Security & control

Strong encryption, resilient infrastructure and pre-trade risk tools help you trade with confidence.
