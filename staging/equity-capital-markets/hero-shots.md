## Brief and read

BRIEF: new hero for Equity Capital Markets (Emirates NBD Capital: IPOs, rights issues, accelerated bookbuilds, listings on the regional exchanges, plus M&A and advisory). Same family as the Insights & Research and smartSCF heroes.

READ: the one moment that belongs only to equity capital markets is listing day: months of readiness, the book built and priced, and then the bell. The picture is the empty exchange hall a few minutes before the first listing ceremony of the morning, the bell lit and waiting, the city outside still blue. One second before, the cleaners have left; one second after, the doors open. No people: the waiting bell is the promise, and it keeps the frame the client's, not a stranger's.

## Treatment

- PLACEMENT: full-bleed page hero, 16:9 plate, `is-bottom` layout, min height about 88vh. Copy sits bottom-left: eyebrow, a long four-line H1, a three-line lead and two CTAs, filling about the left 55 percent of the width and the lower 60 percent of the height at 1440. The overlay is a flat `ov-tint` at 70 percent, the heaviest in the family, so the plate needs one clear warm point that survives the tint, and it must supply its own calm under the copy. The transparent brand header sits over the top tenth.
- HUMAN OR NOT: no people. The subject is a market moment, not a banker. At this tint a face goes muddy anyway, and the family is people-free. Stated as the call.
- GENRE: photographic editorial, matching the family and the page's own photographic cards. No CGI, no tickers, no candlestick charts, no glowing data.
- BRAND MECHANISM (ENBD): colour is the light. Navy and cobalt from blue dawn or dusk through glass; gold from a single warm spotlight or brass practicals. Nothing laid on as a grade.
- LIGHT: cool blue ambient through glass as the fill, one warm source as the accent, about 6:1 so floors and walls fall to navy by themselves.
- CAMERA AND LENS: 35mm feel, f/2.8 for interiors, f/5.6 for the exterior; low or seated eye level so the subject rises into the upper middle of the frame.
- GRADE: navy and cobalt shadows, warm gold highlights, soft contrast, natural colour, fine grain.
- COMPOSITION: the subject and the brightest point sit in a band between about 20 and 45 percent of the height, horizontally between 45 and 62 percent of the width. The lower half, across the full width, is a calm, even, dark surface (stone floor, shadowed table end, plaza). The top tenth is dark ceiling or sky.
- BILINGUAL SOLUTION: centre-weighted subject with type below. The flat tint needs no mirroring and the lower half is calm edge to edge, so one plate serves the LTR copy (bottom-left) and the RTL copy (bottom-right). If the winner drifts past 65 percent of the width, flip it for the Arabic page as a fallback (safe: no text, no handedness cues).
- MOBILE CROP: at 375 the cover crop keeps roughly the central 30 percent of the width (about 35 to 65 percent). Keep the bell, pendant or lobby inside 42 to 60 percent so it survives and shows above the H1.
- REGIONAL FIT: generic contemporary Gulf towers, no identifiable building, no exchange interior that could be read as a real one, no exchange logos, tickers or boards. No religious motifs as decoration. Date palms are fine.
- MODEL: `seedream-5-pro`, 16:9, 2k, matching the sister heroes.
- ROUND: 2 variants per direction, 6 images. Axis under test is the subject only; light, lens, grade and composition are locked. Then 2 more of the winner only if it needs a fix. Export 1920x1080 JPEG q80, under 400 KB, strip metadata; plus a 1200x630 crop for OG.

Prompt style: plain descriptive paragraphs, under 200 words each, per the filter lesson from the last round. No spec blocks, no grading jargon, short closing line.

Ram wants people in the hero. Round 1 kept the frame people-free because a 70 percent flat tint muddies faces; the hero is now getting a lighter left-to-right dark gradient, so faces on the right half survive.
