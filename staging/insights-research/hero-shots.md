# Insights & Research hero, shot sheet (Kiran)

Status 2026-10-13: TREATMENT, ready to shoot. Art direction only; generation runs from the main session.
Target file: `staging/insights-research/insights-hero.jpg` (poster plate for the hero video slot, or a still replacement).

## Brief and read

BRIEF: new hero for Insights & Research, family resemblance to the Media Library press hall, but saying "research and intelligence".

READ: the copy promises a clear view of the whole region's markets, so the picture is the quiet hour when the thinking gets done: an empty, lamp-lit place of study looking out over a city and a working coastline at blue hour. The moment is just before the morning note goes out, the city still blue, one warm lamp on.

Current asset note: the live poster is an aerial of San Francisco and the Bay Bridge. Wrong city for a MENAT research desk, and recognisable. Any of the directions below fixes that.

## Treatment

- PLACEMENT: full-bleed page hero, 16:9, copy bottom-left under a left-to-right dark gradient (80%) and a top dark gradient (50%). Both overlays already darken the left and the top edge.
- HUMAN OR NOT: no people. The subject is an institution's judgement, not a person. A face would pull the eye away from the headline and break the family with the press hall, which is empty. Stated as the call, not a question.
- GENRE: photographic editorial, matching the article cards (Fed building, Treasury, gold, dollar bills) and the press hall. No CGI, no glass-and-hologram "data" clichés, no floating charts.
- BRAND MECHANISM (ENBD): colour comes from the light in the scene. Navy and cobalt from the blue-hour sky and glass, gold from warm tungsten practicals (a brass lamp, track spots). No colour laid on as a grade.
- LIGHT: dusk ambient through floor-to-ceiling glass on the right as the key fill, one or two warm 2700K practicals as the accent pools, about 6:1 ratio so the room falls to navy shadow and the left side goes nearly black on its own.
- CAMERA AND LENS: full-frame, 35mm prime at f/2.8, eye level about 1.3 m, gentle falloff. Same lens feel as the press hall, so the two pages read as one family.
- GRADE: soft contrast, lifted but not milky navy blacks, warm neutral highlights on brass and lamp light, natural saturation, fine grain.
- COMPOSITION: interest weighted to the right half, between one third and two thirds of the height (the top edge is already darkened by the top overlay, so nothing important touches the top tenth). The left third, and especially the lower-left, is calm dark shadow: a near-black wall, a deep column or unlit floor.
- BILINGUAL SOLUTION: mirrored plates. The `ov-gradient-l` overlay does not flip in RTL today, so the Arabic page needs a horizontally flipped copy of the winning plate (`insights-hero-rtl.jpg`) with the overlay mirrored. Flipping is safe because the plates carry no text, no landmarks and no handedness cues. The overlay mirror is a system decision for the main session, flagged not solved here.
- REGIONAL FIT: skyline is generic contemporary Gulf towers, no identifiable landmark. Port and sea are the Gulf, warm haze. Nothing decorative that is religious, no alcohol, no screens with figures.
- MODEL: `seedream-5-pro`, 16:9, 2k. Photoreal is the finish model and the sister hero was shot on it, so the grade will match.
- ROUND: 2 variants per direction, 6 images (about 600 credits at the Media Library rate). Axis under test is the subject only; light, lens, grade and composition are locked across all three. Then 2 more of the winner only if it needs a fix. No upscale needed, 2k covers a 1920 hero; export 1920x1080 JPEG q80, strip metadata, under 400 KB.

## Direction A, The Reading Room (RECOMMENDED)

An empty research library reading room on a high floor at blue hour, photographed as an editorial interior. On the right half of the frame a long dark walnut reading table runs away from camera toward a floor-to-ceiling glass wall, with a single brass banker's lamp glowing warm amber on the table, a few closed navy cloth-bound volumes with blank unprinted spines stacked beside it, a closed leather folio and a fountain pen, and navy leather chairs pushed neatly in. Behind the table, tall walnut shelves of plain cloth-bound volumes in navy, cream and muted gold recede into soft focus, their spines blank and out of focus. Through the glass, a soft out-of-focus skyline of contemporary towers at blue hour, lit windows glowing warm, deep cobalt sky with a faint amber band on the horizon. The left third of the frame is a near-black navy wall and a deep shadowed column, and the lower-left third is calm dark shadow on a dark carpet with no detail. Light: cool blue dusk ambient through the glass from camera-right, the brass lamp and two warm ceiling spots as the only warm sources, strong falloff into shadow, about 6:1 ratio. Camera: full-frame 35mm prime at f/2.8, eye level, gentle depth falloff, the lamp and table edge in sharp focus. Grade: navy and cobalt shadows, warm gold highlights on brass and walnut, soft contrast, lifted navy blacks, natural saturation, fine film grain. Photographic, quiet, still, 16:9.

Avoid: text, letters, words, numbers, captions, typography, watermark, legible screens, charts with numbers, signage, logos, brand marks, recognisable landmarks, people, book titles, printed pages in focus, banknotes, lens flare overload, CGI render look, holograms, floating graphics.

Why it fits: "intelligence" and "white papers" read instantly as a place of study, the lamp is the single point of warmth the eye lands on after the headline, and it shares the press hall's grammar (dark left wall, glass and city on the right, warm practicals) while being a clearly different room.

Risk to watch: book spines and the folio are where fake text appears. Zoom the shelves and table; reroll if any spine or cover carries letterforms.

## Direction B, The Analyst's Desk at First Light

A still life of a single research desk beside a floor-to-ceiling window on a high floor, before dawn, photographed as an editorial interior. On the right half of the frame, a clean dark walnut desk holds a slim closed laptop, a warm brass task lamp switched on, a ceramic cup of Arabic coffee on a saucer, a closed navy leather notebook and a pen, and a small gold bar paperweight with a plain unmarked surface catching the lamplight. The desk sits against the glass, and through it a wide calm view of the Gulf coast below: a container port with tall cranes, a few tankers at anchor on still water, and a city of contemporary towers along the shore, all soft and hazy in deep blue pre-dawn light with the first thin amber line on the horizon. The left third of the frame is a dark navy wall and the edge of an unlit shelving unit, and the lower-left third is calm dark shadow on the floor with no detail. Light: cool blue dawn ambient through the glass from camera-right, the brass task lamp as the only warm source pooling on the desk, about 6:1 ratio. Camera: full-frame 35mm prime at f/2.8, eye level just above desk height, the desk objects sharp and the coastline soft. Grade: navy and cobalt shadows, warm gold highlights on brass and the gold bar, soft contrast, lifted navy blacks, natural saturation, fine film grain. Photographic, quiet, still, 16:9.

Avoid: text, letters, words, numbers, captions, typography, watermark, legible screens, charts with numbers, signage, logos, brand marks, recognisable landmarks, people, book titles, printed pages in focus, banknotes, lens flare overload, CGI render look, holograms, floating graphics.

Why it fits: one desk says "our economists", the port, tankers and gold quietly name the page's beats (trade, oil, gold) without a single chart, and first light reads as "daily insights". Weaker than A at hero scale because the desk objects are small and the story leans on props.

Risk to watch: the gold bar will try to carry an assay stamp with numbers, and the laptop may open itself. Reroll on either; the gold bar is optional and can be dropped from the prompt if it keeps stamping.

## Recommendation

Direction A, The Reading Room. It is the only one whose subject says "research" before anything else, it gives the eye one warm point (the lamp) on the right to land on after the headline, it keeps the press hall's family grammar without repeating its subject, and it is the calmest frame behind a long lead line. C is the fallback if Ram wants the page to read as "markets" rather than "research".
