## Series treatment

- Genre: photographic editorial, matching the page (all existing art is photographic). No CGI.
- Brand mechanism (ENBD): colour comes from the light in the scene. Clear and bright by default, warm dusk for 3 and 10. Navy lives in shadows, glass, suits and evening sky, always paired with a warm second temperature (gold sun, skin, wood, brass, cream stone).
- Camera: full-frame, 35mm prime, f/2.8, eye level, gentle depth falloff. Interiors 35mm, exteriors 35mm, still lifes 50mm at f/2.8. Same lens feel across all 10.
- Grade: clean digital, soft contrast, lifted but not milky blacks with a slight navy bias, warm neutral highlights, natural saturation, fine grain.
- Composition: 3:2. Cards keep the lower fifth calm and uncluttered across the FULL width (chip sits bottom-left in LTR, bottom-right in RTL). Featured keeps the upper fifth calm across the full width, subject centred so it crops safely. Solution: centre-weighted, symmetric quiet band.
- Human or not: people in 1, 3, 6, 9 (stories about clients and community); still life or environment in 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 (institutional ideas read better as objects at 380px card width).
- Casting: ENBD mix, roughly 70 percent international, 30 percent Emirati; modest wardrobe throughout.
- Model: `seedream-5-pro`, 3:2, 2k (about 100 credits each). Round 1: 2 variants per file (20 images, about 2,000 credits; run simulate_cost first). Reroll only failures. No upscale needed: 2k is above the 1600px target. Export: resize to 1600px long edge, JPEG q82, strip metadata, under 600 KB.
