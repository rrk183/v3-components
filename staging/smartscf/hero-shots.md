## Brief and read

BRIEF: new hero for the smartSCF page (digital supply chain finance: suppliers paid early, payables and distributor receivables financed; buyers, suppliers, distributors; retail and FMCG, manufacturing, distribution, healthcare). Same family as the Insights & Research hero.

READ: the headline is "Pay suppliers early. Free up working capital." The picture is what that money looks like when it lands: a supply chain that keeps moving. The moment is the evening dispatch at a Gulf distribution centre, blue hour, every dock door open and lit, goods leaving on time. One second before, the last trailer reversed in; one second after, the next door rolls up. No people needed: the open, glowing doors are the proof that the chain is flowing.

## Treatment

- PLACEMENT: full-bleed page hero, 16:9, `is-bottom` layout. Copy is bottom-left: eyebrow, two-line H1, a three-line lead and two CTAs, filling roughly the left half of the frame and the lower 55 percent of its height. The overlay is a flat uniform `ov-tint` at 60 percent (no gradients), so the plate itself must supply the calm under the copy. The transparent brand header also sits over the top tenth.
- HUMAN OR NOT: no people. The subject is flow, not a person; a worker at hero scale becomes the subject and brings hi-vis, hard hats and casting risk. Stated as the call.
- GENRE: photographic, matching the family (press hall, Insights reading room) and the page's own photographic cards. No CGI, no network lines, no floating icons, no holograms.
- BRAND MECHANISM (ENBD): colour is the light. Navy and cobalt from the blue-hour sky and cool ambient on concrete; gold from warm 3000K tungsten and sodium practicals spilling out of the dock doors. No grade laid on.
- LIGHT: blue-hour sky as the soft fill, warm interior light through the open dock doors as the key accents, about 6:1 so concrete and tarmac fall to deep navy on their own.
- CAMERA AND LENS: full-frame 35mm prime, f/4 for the exteriors (more depth than the interiors in the family, because the building is the subject), eye level about 1.5 m, verticals corrected.
- GRADE: soft contrast, lifted navy blacks, warm neutral highlights, natural saturation, fine grain. Same as Insights.
- COMPOSITION: interest lives in a horizontal band between roughly 15 and 50 percent of the height, centre to right, with the brightest point between 50 and 70 percent of the width. The lower half of the frame, across its full width, is a calm, unlit, near-uniform dark surface (tarmac, polished concrete, desert ground). The top tenth is plain dark sky or ceiling.
- BILINGUAL SOLUTION: centre-weighted subject with type below. Because the lower half is calm across the whole width and the overlay is a flat tint that needs no mirroring, one plate serves both the LTR copy (bottom-left) and the RTL copy (bottom-right). No mirrored plate required. If the winner comes back with its brightest point drifting past 75 percent of the width, flip it for RTL as a fallback (safe: no text, no handedness cues).
- MOBILE CROP: at 375 the cover crop keeps roughly the central third of the width, and the copy covers most of the height. Keep the brightest door or light pool inside 35 to 70 percent of the width so it survives the crop and shows above the H1.
- REGIONAL FIT: low desert-tone industrial architecture, warm haze, palms or a pale sand horizon are fine; generic, no landmark. Plain unmarked white trailers. No alcohol or restricted goods visible; cartons are plain kraft or white shrink-wrap.
- MODEL: `seedream-5-pro`, 16:9, 2k, same as the sister hero so the grade matches.
- ROUND: 2 variants per direction, 6 images. Axis under test is the subject only; light, lens, grade and composition are locked. Then 2 more of the winner only if it needs a fix. Export 1920x1080 JPEG q80, under 400 KB, strip metadata; plus 1200x630 for OG.

## Direction A, Evening Dispatch (RECOMMENDED)

A modern regional distribution centre on the edge of a Gulf city at blue hour, photographed from ground level across its wide loading apron as an editorial architecture photograph. The long low building runs across the middle of the frame, its pale sand-coloured insulated cladding turned cool blue in the dusk. A row of eight tall dock doors is rolled fully open, each one a rectangle of warm golden tungsten light revealing the interior: neat rows of plain shrink-wrapped pallets and kraft cartons on tall racking, softly out of focus. Three plain unmarked white box trailers are backed squarely into the docks in the centre and right of the row, their rear doors open into the light. The brightest open doors sit just right of centre. Above the building, a clean deep cobalt sky fading to a thin amber band on the horizon, with two slim light poles and a faint distant line of low towers in haze at the far right. The lower half of the frame is the empty loading apron, smooth dark asphalt with a faint satin sheen, calm and near-uniform in deep navy shadow, with only a soft warm reflection of the doors at its far edge and no markings. The top tenth is plain dark blue sky. Light: blue-hour sky as soft fill, warm tungsten spilling from the open doors as the only warm source, about 6:1 ratio. Camera: full-frame 35mm prime at f/4, eye level, verticals corrected, building in sharp focus. Grade: navy and cobalt shadows, warm gold highlights in the doorways, soft contrast, lifted navy blacks, natural saturation, fine film grain. Photographic, calm, orderly, 16:9.

Avoid: text, letters, words, numbers, captions, typography, watermark, legible screens, charts with numbers, signage, logos, brand marks, container markings, ship names, trailer graphics, licence plates, carton labels, barcodes, recognisable landmarks, people, hard hats, hi-vis vests, CGI render look, holograms, floating icons, network lines, lens flare overload.

Why it fits: open, lit doors with trailers backed in say "the chain is moving" at a glance, which is the payoff of paying suppliers early. It is the single frame that covers every sector on the page (retail, FMCG, manufacturing, healthcare all ship from a dock like this), it is new to the site, and it carries the family grammar exactly: blue-hour navy, gold practicals, a quiet dark foreground. The apron gives a naturally dark, even lower half for the copy in both reading directions.

Risk to watch: dock doors love painted numbers and trailers love livery. Zoom every door header and trailer side; reroll on any digit or mark. Also check the apron stays free of painted bay lines with numbers.
