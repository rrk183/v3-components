What Emirates NBD Pay is, how it integrates, and how to keep your business safe on it.

Emirates NBD Pay is our payment acceptance platform for in-store and online channels. It supports card payments in more than 143 currencies and enables chip and PIN, contactless, QR and digital wallet transactions. For online businesses, Emirates NBD Pay offers secure e-commerce gateways with tokenisation, fraud controls and configurable checkout flows. You can issue payment links and track transactions through a unified dashboard. These capabilities support merchants serving global customer bases.

Yes. Emirates NBD Pay can integrate with most leading ERP and treasury management systems via automated data exchange or manual uploads. This ensures smooth invoice submission, approval, and payment reconciliation without disrupting existing workflows.

When using businessONLINE, smartTRADE, smartSCF or Emirates NBD Pay, access platforms only through official URLs, review payment details before approval, apply role-based access, disable inactive users, and enable two-factor authentication. Avoid approving transactions over public Wi-Fi. Strong internal governance is your most effective safeguard.
