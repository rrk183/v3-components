Digital Solutions : smartGUARANTEES

Receive and manage all your bank guarantees online.

Receive, amend, extend or track your guarantees in a completely digital and seamless manner.

Talk to your Relationship Manager See how it works
What it is

The digital home for your guarantees.

Enjoy the digital bank-guarantee capability inside smartTRADE, Emirates NBD's online trade-finance platform. Handle the full life of a guarantee, from request to closure, without paper.

On the platform

Covering all your needs of a guarantee, in one place.

From the first request to the final claim, every action on a guarantee happens on the same screen, with a full history behind it.

View online

See every guarantee and its transaction details in real time.

Amend, extend or cancel

Change terms, push the expiry, or close a guarantee, digitally.

Raise claims

Submit and manage claims through the same platform.

Advised over SWIFT

Guarantees are routed over SWIFT to be advised to beneficiary banks.

Upload documents

Attach supporting files in PDF, Word, Excel or image formats.

Reusable clauses

Store wording you use often and reapply it in seconds.

Real-time status

Track where each request stands at every step.

Reporting dashboard

See due dates, outstanding items and history at a glance.

Guarantee coverage

Cover for every guarantee you issue or receive.

Whether you are giving a guarantee or receiving one, Emirates NBD covers the full range, from the shipping guarantees your trade flows rely on to counter guarantees.

Outward guarantees

Issued by Emirates NBD on your behalf, in favour of your beneficiary.

Inward guarantees

Received in your favour and advised to you through the bank.

Shipping guarantee

Take delivery of goods before the shipping documents arrive.

Counter guarantees

Issued to another bank so it can issue a guarantee to your beneficiary abroad.

The difference

Less paper, less waiting, more control.

Fewer forms to sign, fewer calls to make, and a clear view of everything you hold.

  • Paperless from request to closure
  • Faster turnaround on issuance and amendments
  • Fewer manual updates and follow-ups
  • Full transparency over every guarantee you hold
Proven at scale

A public-sector deployment.

In 2021, Emirates NBD partnered with Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority to digitise its bank-guarantee process, removing paper from those transactions entirely.

Emirates NBD and Dubai RTA, 2021.
