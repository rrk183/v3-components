# cards-kicker

`cards-kicker` · Cards · composition of **[[cards]]**

Kicker composition of [[cards]] — a word kicker (type-eyebrow type-blue) + title + body in a raised card-panel; replaces kicker-cards; see the family dossier.

## Options
- **Photo ground:** the section may sit on `surface-image` with a `data-bg` photo (and an `ov` wash); cards then use a dark-ground card surface.
- **Closing button row:** one centred button under the grid.
- **Compound kicker:** the kicker may combine a number and a word, e.g. "01 · Structuring".
- **Card surface:** `surface-clear` panels (gallery Card surface control).
- **Kicker optional** (2026-10-12): the `type-eyebrow` kicker may be dropped on every card (all or none), leaving title + body in the raised panel.
- **Anchor id (instances only):** a page may add `id="..."` to the root.
