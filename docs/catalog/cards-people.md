# cards-people

`cards-people` · Cards · composition of **[[cards]]**

People composition of [[cards]] — portrait whole-card links (taller `--cd-min`), ov-gradient-b wash, name + role + View profile pill; replaces people-grid / people-carousel (pair with cmp-modal .op-modal for bios); see the family dossier.

## Options
- **Pop-up profiles** (2026-10-12): a card may open a biography modal (`href="#" data-modal-open="<id>"`) instead of linking to a page.
- **Any column count:** `is-cols-2/3/4`, or pass `style="--cd-cols: 5"` for five (the cmp-cards column variable).
- **Anchor id (instances only):** a page may add `id="..."` to the root.
