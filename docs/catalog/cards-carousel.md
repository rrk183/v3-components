# cards-carousel

`cards-carousel` · Cards · composition of **[[cards]]**

Carousel layout of [[cards]] — same cards on the shared data-carousel recipe; see the family dossier.

## Options
- **Manual mode** (2026-10-12): drop `data-autoplay` (and the `crl-toggle` pause button with it); arrows, dots and swipe still work.
- **Static cards:** cards may be `div`s instead of links, and the corner `chip` tags are optional (all cards or none).
- **Header side link:** any Section Header option applies, including a lead + action pair in `.sh-aside`.
- **Anchor id (instances only):** a page may add `id="..."` to the root.
