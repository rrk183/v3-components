# cards-people-carousel

`cards-people-carousel` · Cards · composition of **[[cards]]**

Carousel layout of the People composition of [[cards]] — the same cards on the shared data-carousel recipe; see the family dossier.

## Options
- **Tabbed groups** (2026-10-12): two or more people carousels can share one header, switched by `chip` tabs (e.g. Leaders / Experts); each group keeps its own arrows and dots.
- **Static profiles:** cards may be `div`s instead of links.
- **Manual mode:** no `data-autoplay`; the pause button then never shows.
