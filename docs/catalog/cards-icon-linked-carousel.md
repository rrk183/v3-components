# cards-icon-linked-carousel

`cards-icon-linked-carousel` · Cards · composition of **[[cards]]**

Carousel layout of the Icon Linked composition of [[cards]] — the same cards on the shared data-carousel recipe; see the family dossier.

## Options
- **Static cards** (2026-10-12): cards may be `div`s instead of links (no arrow link then).
- **Manual mode:** no `data-autoplay`, no pause button.
- **Closing button row:** one centred `btn btn-filled btn-lg` under the carousel.
